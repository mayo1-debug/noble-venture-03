Questions

Is this a certification or legal advice?

No. The kit helps you describe your real security practices clearly and gives you templates to write them down. It does not make you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified, and it is not legal advice. Your answers must match what you actually do.

Does my data leave my browser?

No. Your profile is stored in your browser's local storage on this device. The site has no server that receives it. Payment details go to PayPal only.

The client uses their own spreadsheet. Does this still help?

Yes. Questions are worded differently in every questionnaire, but they ask about the same controls. Find the matching answer in the kit, copy it, or export all answers as CSV and paste them into the client's sheet.

What if I answered "no" to many controls?

The kit writes honest answers for missing controls, with your target date, and the gap plan lists the fix steps. Most fixes for a small team are settings you can change in an hour (MFA, disk encryption, automatic updates).
